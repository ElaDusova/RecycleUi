Simplifying Product Recycle

We help people recycle products efficiently using EAN codes. Our system makes your recycling easier and our world more ecological.

Our Mission

Our goal is to develop an intuitive platform that automates and simplifies the process recycling products based on their EAN codes. By leveraging modern technologies, we help reduce waste, and enhance productivity.

Our Vision

We envision a world where recycling is fully optimized through smart automation. By providing a user-friendly and powerful platform, we want people to know recycling is now a part of our life and we should mak it publicly known.

How It Works

Our system simplifies product categorization by scanning and processing EAN codes, automatically organizing items into predefined categories. This ensures smooth and precise decisions in your recycling.

1. Scan EAN Code

Quickly input or scan product codes to retrieve information.

2. Automatic Sorting

Our system categorizes products based on predefined logic.

3. Learn & Recycle!

Easily find informations, read, and RECYCLE your product.
